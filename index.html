03 — Club Activities & Projects

What our club does every term

Lestari Alam Club activities are planned around school routines, classroom responsibilities, and special environmental programmes guided by teachers.

Why are these club activities important?

  • Shapes ecological responsibility from a young age through action, not just theory.
  • Builds applied STEM skills — measurement, data analysis, and problem solving.
  • Strengthens the club ↔ school relationship through shared work with real benefits.
  • Contributes directly to UN SDGs 4, 11, 12 & 13.

        06 — Join the Club

        Green habits start with one member.

        The Lestari Alam Club is open to SK Bukit Damansara students who want to take part in recycling, gardening, composting, and environmental awareness activities.

        1. 1
          Register as a club member
